AsistenDiet started from one observation: people fail to keep their diet not because they lack knowledge, but because logging feels like extra work.

The product moves that work into conversation. Users send a message or a photo of their meal, the system analyses its nutritional content, and returns feedback tied to the goal they picked during onboarding — losing weight, maintaining, or gaining mass.

That approach demands three things at once: reliable food-data extraction, a consistent assistant voice, and retention. A health product is not a one-time transaction, so onboarding, reminders, and progress have to reinforce each other.
