## The problem

Assignment letters (*surat tugas*) and official travel at a large university used to move on paper: requests, approvals at several levels, signatures, then reports afterwards. That was slow to process and hard to audit.

## What I built

A single digital flow that is recorded and verifiable from request to report:

- **Multilevel approvals** with role-based access for staff, lecturers and students across the university's units.
- **Electronic signatures** and **automated PDF generation** for official letters.
- **Public document verification**, so anyone can confirm that a letter is genuine.
- **Geo-verified reporting** with photo watermarking for proof of activity.
- **Payment-gateway integration** and audit-oriented backend workflows for expenses.

The same architecture also serves the university's expense-workflow and legal-document systems.

## Impact

More than **15,000 assignment letters** have been issued through the system, for units across the university.
